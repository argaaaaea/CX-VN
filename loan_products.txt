1. VAY ỨNG LỰC CỘNG (SALARY ADVANCE LOAN) (ID: ABVN-SAL-001)

The Salary Advance Loan, known in Vietnamese as "Vay Ứng Lực Cộng," is a quick personal loan designed specifically for employees who receive monthly salaries. This product is tailored for salaried workers across Vietnam who need immediate access to funds. The loan amount ranges from 10,000,000 to 500,000,000 VND (approximately USD $410 to $20,500), with interest rates between 6.5% and 11.5% per annum. Borrowers can choose from flexible repayment terms ranging from 6 to 60 months. The processing fee is set at 1% of the loan amount, making it an affordable option for quick borrowing.

To be eligible for this loan, applicants must be between 21 and 60 years old with a minimum monthly income of 5,000,000 VND (approximately $205). Applicants need to have an employment contract that has been in place for at least 6 months. The bank will review the applicant's credit score equivalent during the approval process. The key features of this loan product include quick approval that can be completed within 1 to 2 business days, automatic salary deduction for repayment which ensures timely payment, and no collateral requirement. Additionally, government workers in Vietnam are eligible for preferential interest rates, making this an attractive option for civil servants and public sector employees.

2. VAY MUA NHÀ - HOME LOAN (MORTGAGE) (ID: ABVN-HOM-002)

The Home Loan, referred to as "Vay Mua Nhà" in Vietnamese, is designed for individuals and families looking to purchase residential properties, build new homes, or invest in real estate throughout Vietnam. This mortgage product is one of the bank's most comprehensive offerings, supporting the significant goal of homeownership for Vietnamese citizens. The loan amounts available range from 200,000,000 to 3,000,000,000 VND (approximately USD $8,200 to $123,000), making it accessible for properties in major urban centers as well as developing areas. The interest rates are competitive, ranging from 4.5% to 8.5% per annum, with extended repayment terms of 120 to 360 months (10 to 30 years) to spread payments over the property's lifetime.

The processing fee for home loans is only 0.5% of the loan amount, which is significantly lower than personal loans to encourage homeownership. To qualify for this mortgage product, applicants must be between 21 and 65 years old with a minimum annual income of 120,000,000 VND (approximately $4,920). The property must be located in major cities such as Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, Da Nang, or other approved municipalities, and the applicant must have a credit score equivalent of 680 or higher. The distinctive features of this home loan include options for both floating and fixed interest rates to suit different borrower preferences, access to government-backed housing programs that provide additional benefits, fast disbursal options for construction phases allowing borrowers to pay as they build, and particularly competitive rates reserved for young families and first-time homebuyers to support the Vietnamese government's homeownership initiative.

3. VAY KINH DOANH NHỎ - SMALL BUSINESS LOAN (ID: ABVN-SMB-003)

The Small Business Loan, known as "Vay Kinh Doanh Nhỏ," is specifically designed for small business owners, shop proprietors, food vendors, and retail store operators throughout Vietnam. This product addresses the unique financing needs of Vietnam's vibrant small business sector, which is crucial to the country's economy. The loan amount ranges from 20,000,000 to 500,000,000 VND (approximately USD $820 to $20,500), providing sufficient capital for inventory, equipment, or expansion purposes. Interest rates for this product are between 7% and 13% per annum, reflecting the moderate risk profile of small businesses, and repayment terms range from 12 to 72 months, allowing business owners to align payments with their cash flow cycles.

The processing fee is set at 2% of the loan amount, which includes administrative costs for business verification. To be eligible, applicants must have been operating their business for at least 1 year and demonstrate a minimum annual revenue of 200,000,000 VND (approximately $8,200). Applicants must provide a valid business registration certificate and proof of tax compliance. Notable features of this small business loan include simple and straightforward documentation requirements that don't burden busy business owners, flexible payment options available on weekly or monthly schedules to match business income patterns, and no collateral requirement for loans under 100 million VND, making it accessible to vendors and small shop owners. Additionally, A Bank Vietnam has developed partnerships with microfinance institutions to provide additional support services and business development advice to small borrowers.

4. VAY NỀN NÔNG NGHIỆP - AGRICULTURAL/FARMING LOAN (ID: ABVN-AGR-004)

The Agricultural and Farming Loan, referred to as "Vay Nền Nông Nghiệp," is a specialized product designed to support Vietnam's agricultural sector, including farmers, aquaculture operators, and plantation owners. This product recognizes the importance of agriculture in Vietnam's economy and the seasonal nature of farming operations. The loan amount ranges from 15,000,000 to 2,000,000,000 VND (approximately USD $615 to $82,000), providing adequate financing for both small-scale and large-scale farming operations. Interest rates are particularly attractive, ranging from 5% to 10% per annum, with government-subsidized rates available to eligible agricultural producers, making farming more profitable and sustainable.

The processing fee is minimal at only 0.5% to 1% of the loan amount, reflecting A Bank Vietnam's commitment to supporting the agricultural sector. To qualify, applicants must possess land ownership or lease certificates, be actively engaged in farming operations, provide proof of farm income, and be between 18 and 70 years old. The key features of this agricultural loan include seasonal payment flexibility, allowing farmers to align loan payments with harvest cycles and seasonal income fluctuations. Crop insurance integration is available, protecting both the borrower and the bank from crop failures and natural disasters. A Bank Vietnam actively participates in government subsidy programs for agriculture, passing these benefits to qualified borrowers. The product also supports livestock financing and equipment purchases for farm mechanization, and special low rates are offered during harvest months to encourage agricultural productivity.

5. VAY MUA XE MÁY/MOTO - MOTORCYCLE/MOTORBIKE LOAN (ID: ABVN-MOT-005)

The Motorcycle and Motorbike Loan, known as "Vay Mua Xe Máy/Moto," is designed for individuals throughout Vietnam who want to purchase motorcycles, motorbikes, or scooters, which are among the most popular forms of personal transportation in the country. This product recognizes the essential role of two-wheeled vehicles in Vietnamese daily life and commerce. The loan amount ranges from 5,000,000 to 150,000,000 VND (approximately USD $205 to $6,150), accommodating purchases of various motorcycle models and brands available in the Vietnamese market. Interest rates are competitive at 4.5% to 9% per annum, and repayment terms range from 12 to 48 months, allowing buyers to pay off their vehicles while using them.

The processing fee is minimal at only 0.75% of the loan amount. To be eligible, applicants must be at least 18 years old, possess a valid Vietnamese driver's license, have a minimum monthly income of 3,000,000 VND (approximately $123), and have permanent residence in Vietnam. The outstanding features of this motorcycle loan include the vehicle itself serving as collateral, providing security for the bank. Same-day approval is available for qualified applicants, enabling quick purchases. An optional insurance bundle is available, providing comprehensive coverage and peace of mind for motorcycle owners. Notably, approved customers can take advantage of zero down payment options, making motorcycle ownership accessible to more Vietnamese citizens. A Bank Vietnam maintains a nationwide dealer network partnership, allowing borrowers to purchase from authorized dealers across the country and receive special financing incentives.

6. VAY XUẤT KHẨU - EXPORT BUSINESS LOAN (ID: ABVN-EXP-006)

The Export Business Loan, called "Vay Xuất Khẩu," is a specialized financing product designed to support Vietnam's exporters, traders, and international business operators. As Vietnam's export sector is vital to the national economy, this product is tailored to meet the unique needs of export-focused enterprises. The loan amount ranges from 100,000,000 to 5,000,000,000 VND (approximately USD $4,100 to $205,000), providing substantial capital for export operations, inventory, or working capital. The interest rates are highly competitive, ranging from 4% to 8.5% per annum, reflecting the lower risk of export-oriented businesses, and repayment terms extend from 12 to 120 months to accommodate business cycles.

The processing fee is 1.5% of the loan amount. To qualify, applicants must be registered exporters with a minimum annual export revenue of 500,000,000 VND (approximately $20,500), have maintained tax compliance for at least 2 years, and maintain a credit score equivalent of 700 or higher. The features of this export loan include competitive interest rates that support export competitiveness in international markets. Trade financing is available to support the purchase of goods for export. The bank offers letter of credit support to facilitate international transactions and reduce payment risks. Flexible repayment terms can be adjusted for seasonal export variations, such as higher payment capacity during peak export seasons. Working capital support is available to maintain operations between shipments, ensuring smooth export operations.

The Education Loan, referred to as "Vay Giáo Dục," is designed to support Vietnamese students and their families in pursuing higher education, vocational training, and professional development. This product reflects A Bank Vietnam's commitment to investing in human capital and supporting education throughout the country. The loan amount ranges from 10,000,000 to 300,000,000 VND (approximately USD $410 to $12,300), covering tuition fees, living expenses, materials, and related educational costs for both domestic and international education. The interest rates are among the most favorable offered by A Bank, ranging from 3.5% to 7% per annum, and repayment terms extend from 24 to 120 months, allowing graduates to repay loans after establishing their careers.

To be eligible, applicants must be at least 18 years old, possess an admission letter or enrollment proof from an accredited institution, have a sponsor with a minimum monthly income of 2,500,000 VND (approximately $102), and maintain a credit score equivalent of 600 or higher. The key features of this education loan include grace period options that allow borrowers to defer payments while still studying. The loan covers both domestic Vietnamese educational institutions and international universities, supporting students who pursue education abroad. Vocational training programs are supported, recognizing that career development includes diverse educational pathways. A Bank Vietnam has established government partnerships to provide additional educational support services and career counseling to borrowers, helping graduates transition smoothly into the workforce.

Equipment warranties are included with the loan package, protecting the borrower's investment in solar technology. Long repayment terms ensure that loan payments are offset by energy savings over time. GENERAL A BANK POLICIES - CHÍNH SÁCH A BANK:

All loan products offered by A Bank Vietnam are subject to credit approval and verification procedures to ensure responsible lending. Prepayment without penalty is available for all products, meaning borrowers can pay off their loans early without incurring additional fees, encouraging borrowers to reduce interest costs. The interest rates shown are representative rates and may vary based on the individual credit profile, income verification, collateral type, and current market conditions. Collateral requirements vary significantly by product and loan amount, with some products requiring no collateral while others may use vehicles, property, or other assets as security. Additional documentation may be required for foreign nationals or those with non-standard income sources. The late payment penalty is structured at 0.05% per day on any overdue amount or a minimum of 150,000 VND, whichever is greater, encouraging timely payments. Travel insurance options are available for travel loans, protecting against trip cancellation or emergency situations abroad. Life insurance options are available for all loan products, providing peace of mind to borrowers and their families. Mobile banking and online application services are available for most products, enabling convenient access to banking services from anywhere in Vietnam.
